# V16 Detail Depth & Color System

## What changed
- Standardized detailed program pages so sparse MEDICAL ACCESS entries no longer collapse to hero + audience + notice.
- `program-detail.js` now falls back to `steps` when `service_scope` is absent.
- Expanded all 14 program data records with a baseline of overview, service scope and Private Journey File; specialty pages now include detailed phases and consultation focus where supported.
- Signature-only `PRIVATE SERVICE LEVELS` remain conditional and are not invented for medical-access pages without a source-defined tier structure.
- Reworked program-detail color hierarchy to espresso / warm ivory / champagne only.
- Converted `PRIVATE JOURNEY` into one controlled dark editorial chapter for visual rhythm.
- Converted MEDICAL NOTICE from a stretched beige warning box into a compact dark signature panel.

## Editorial/source rule
- at PRIVÉ is described as a non-medical concierge/navigation layer. Medical diagnosis, testing, treatment and procedural decisions remain with the medical institution and physicians.
- Public hospital material may inform factual categories and service mechanics, but wording is paraphrased and must not imply ownership, formal partnership, exclusive access, or that at PRIVÉ itself provides a hospital's facilities/services.
